Brew Better

The Perfect Cup, Every Time

✦

Follow our master barista extraction guides to unlock the full potential of your single-origin beans at home and at CAFFYO.

1
Grind Fine & Precise

Use a fine grind with table salt consistency. Freshly ground single-origin Arabica is essential.

2
Dose & Tamp Firmly

Dose 18–20g of ground coffee into a clean portafilter. Tamp evenly with 30 lbs of level pressure.

3
9-Bar Precision Extraction

Target 25–28 seconds for a rich 36–40ml double shot at 93°C with dense golden crema.

4
Savor Promptly

Espresso is alive — enjoy within 60 seconds or fold into microfoam for silky swan latte art.

METHOD: 9-BAR MANUAL TIME: 25–30 SEC
1
Medium-Fine Grind

Coarse beach sand consistency. 22g of Estate Blonde roast per 350ml of 93°C mineral water.

2
The 45-Second Bloom

Pour 50ml hot water gently over grounds and wait 45 seconds for carbon dioxide degassing.

3
Slow Spiral Pouring

Pour remaining water in gentle outward spirals without washing down the filter paper walls.

4
Total Drawdown: 3:15

Swirl server gently before pouring into pre-warmed ceramic cup. Note delicate jasmine & citrus aromatics.

METHOD: V60 DRIPPER TIME: 3 MIN 15 SEC
1
Coarse Sea Salt Grind

Coarse grind prevents fine sediment and avoids over-extraction bitterness. 30g coffee to 500ml water.

2
Pour & 4-Minute Immersion

Saturate grounds completely with 95°C water. Stir once gently, replace lid, and steep undisturbed for 4 minutes.

3
Break & Skim Crust

Gently break the surface crust with a spoon and skim away top foam for an ultra-clean mouthfeel.

4
Gentle Even Plunge

Press the mesh filter down with steady, gentle resistance. Decant immediately into mugs.

METHOD: FULL IMMERSION TIME: 4 MINUTES
1
Dutch Tower Grind

Consistent medium-coarse grind placed in glass extraction chamber with ceramic dispersion disc.

2
Purified Ice Water Chamber

Fill top glass chamber with mineral ice and purified chilled water at 3°C.

3
18-Hour Drop-By-Drop

Calibrate brass valve to precisely 1 drop every 1.5 seconds. Zero heat preserves volatile floral esters.

4
Poured Over Crystal Rock

Serve neat over crystal-clear artisan ice rock for an extraordinarily smooth, winey, low-acid nectar.
